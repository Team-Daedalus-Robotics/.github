## About us

Team Daedalus Robotics is a team that brings together robotics, software and electronics to build practical
solutions. In 2026 we took part in the **NASA Space Apps Challenge** to apply that experience to agriculture.

## Featured project: SAERPA

**SAERPA** is our project for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, in the challenge
*Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data*.

It helps small farmers decide **what to plant next** in each plot. It combines:

- **NASA Earth observation data** (POWER climate and SMAP soil moisture),
- **low-cost field sensors**, and
- **agronomic knowledge** (the Embrapa planting calendar and crop rotation rules),

and turns them into plain-language recommendations in Portuguese. It needs no login and keeps the map
available when the connection drops.

**[Explore the SAERPA repository](https://github.com/Team-Daedalus-Robotics/saerpa)**
